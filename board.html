Curators' board

Where each submission stands: what has been recorded on it, and what it is waiting on. It needs the curators' password.

    The board is written from the review ledger each time the intake programs run, so a verdict recorded on the form appears here after the next run, not at once. The email you get when a verdict is recorded is the immediate notice.

    It is decrypted in your browser; the password is not sent anywhere. It shows no submitter's name and no reason for any verdict: those are in the report and in the emails between curators.
